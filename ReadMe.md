General Instructions:
Environment Setup
Choose a Quiet Space: Find a well-lit, quiet environment with minimal distractions.
Device Placement: Position your smartphone or webcam to clearly capture both your face and hands.

Recording Preparation
Open Your Camera App: Use a reliable video recording app on your device.
Landscape Mode: Set the device to record in landscape orientation for better video quality.
Frame the Shot: Ensure both your face and hands are visible within the frame.
*Hide the Non-Visible Hand from the Frame*

Start Recording: Begin recording.
Perform the Task:
